## Spatial-distance-controlled high/low CoView experiment

The controlled pool contains 1,280,000 pairs from 20,000 sampled anchors and their 64 nearest spatial neighbours. High/low comparison retained 267,208 same-source matched pairs whose distance mismatch is at most one codec voxel (0.005). Retained pairs were split into 10 distance-quantile bins.

- feature_cosine: expected direction in 0/10 bins; mean(high-low)=-0.005085
- feature_l2: expected direction in 1/10 bins; mean(high-low)=0.062870
- scaling_l2: expected direction in 10/10 bins; mean(high-low)=-0.002090
- offset_l2: expected direction in 2/10 bins; mean(high-low)=0.040817

High/low CoView sets are formed separately within each source anchor's 64-NN pool. Every high-CoView pair is matched to the nearest-distance low-CoView pair from the same source anchor and rejected if the mismatch exceeds the caliper. `controlled_high_low.csv` records the matched mean distances, mean absolute distance mismatch, source count, and unique matched-low count. Per-bin correlations over the full unfiltered pool are in `distance_binned_correlations.csv`.

## Phase 2 recommendation

**暂不建议直接进入 Phase 2.** The descriptive gate used here requires mean Spatial/CoView overlap below 0.80 and the controlled feature cosine or feature-L2 direction to hold in at least 60% of valid distance bins. This is a reporting aid, not a p-value or a fixed scientific significance threshold; cross-scene consistency is still required.
